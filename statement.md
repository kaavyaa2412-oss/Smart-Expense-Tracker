# Smart Expense Tracker

## 1. Problem Statement

Managing daily expenses manually can be difficult and time-consuming. People often lose track of where their money is being spent, how much they have spent, and whether they are staying within their monthly budget.

The Smart Expense Tracker is developed to provide a simple Java-based solution for recording, viewing, searching, and analyzing expenses. It helps users calculate their total spending and monitor their monthly budget through a simple menu-driven interface.

---

## 2. Scope of the Project

The scope of the Smart Expense Tracker includes:

- Recording daily expenses.
- Storing expense details temporarily using Java ArrayList.
- Viewing all recorded expenses.
- Searching expenses based on categories.
- Calculating total expenses.
- Finding the highest expense.
- Calculating the average expense.
- Setting a monthly budget.
- Checking the remaining or exceeded budget.
- Deleting unwanted expense records.
- Displaying an overall expense summary.

The current version is a console-based Java application and does not use SQL or an external database.

---

## 3. Target Users

The application is mainly designed for:

- Students who want to manage their daily spending.
- Individuals who want to track personal expenses.
- Beginners learning Java programming.
- Users who need a simple budget monitoring tool.
- Anyone who wants to maintain a basic record of their expenses.

---

## 4. High-Level Features

### 1. Add Expense
Users can enter the expense title, category, amount, and date.

### 2. View Expenses
Users can view all recorded expenses along with their details.

### 3. Search Expense
Users can search for expenses by category such as Food, Transport, Education, or Shopping.

### 4. Expense Calculation
The application calculates the total amount spent and the average expense.

### 5. Highest Expense
The application identifies the expense with the highest amount.

### 6. Budget Management
Users can set a monthly budget and monitor their spending against it.

### 7. Budget Status
The application displays the remaining budget or the amount by which the budget has been exceeded.

### 8. Delete Expense
Users can delete an expense by entering its unique ID.

### 9. Expense Summary
The application provides a summary containing the number of expenses, total expenses, average expense, monthly budget, and remaining budget.

---

## 5. Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to develop a simple and user-friendly expense management application while applying fundamental Java programming concepts such as classes, objects, constructors, methods, ArrayList, loops, conditional statements, and switch-case.
